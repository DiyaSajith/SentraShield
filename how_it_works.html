Intelligent Data Ingestion

Whether you paste a URL, a text message, or upload a screenshot, our system first identifies the data type.

• URLs are normalized and domains extracted.
• Images undergo OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to extract text and links.
• QR Codes are decoded to reveal their payload.

Threat Intelligence Query

We query trusted global databases like VirusTotal and Google Safe Browsing. We check:

  • Is the domain blacklisted?
  • Has the URL been reported for phishing?
  • Is the IP address associated with botnets?

AI Contextual Analysis

This is where CyberGuard shines. Our LLM (Large Language Model) analyzes the *semantics* of the content.

It reads the text of a message to detect urgency ("Act now!"), threats ("Your account will be deleted"), and impersonation attempts, recognizing patterns that traditional scanners miss.

Unified Risk Report

We synthesize all findings into a single Risk Score (0-100). You get a clear "Malicious", "Suspicious", or "Safe" verdict, along with a plain-English summary explaining exactly *why* that conclusion was reached.
